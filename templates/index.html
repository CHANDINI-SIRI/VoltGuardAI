    📋 Mandatory Inspection Checklist for Electrician

    Show this checklist to the electrician so they test these exact 5 points:

    • 1. Clamp Meter Earth-Leakage Test: Turn OFF indoor switches and measure Phase vs. Neutral current at main MCB (>30mA confirms wall leak).
    • 2. Neutral-to-Earth Voltage Test: Check socket Neutral-to-Earth voltage (must be <3V; higher means loose neutral joint).
    • 3. Peak Load Balancing: Check why Recorded MD spiked above sanctioned load and balance heavy appliances.
    • 4. Geyser, Fridge & Inverter Check: Verify thermostat auto-cutoff and inverter charging draw.
    • 5. Outdoor Service Cable Check: Inspect overhead cable from pole to meter box for worn insulation or wire tapping.
